# Face landmark models

`face_detector.tflite` (BlazeFace short-range) and `face_landmarks_detector.tflite` (Face Mesh, 478 landmarks) are the
unmodified TFLite models from Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker bundle
(https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/face_landmarker/face_landmarker/float16/1/face_landmarker.task),
released under the Apache License 2.0. They run locally through `ai-edge-litert`; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
